What's Shifting

Across eLearning, AEC, and technical documentation — the three worlds zipBoard quietly sits inside — the act of "reviewing" is being rewritten in real time. Recent conversations around SCORM review tools, drawing approval workflows, and technical writer toolkits all point in the same direction: stakeholders no longer want a faster way to leave comments, they want the comments pre-drafted, triaged, and routed before a human ever opens the file. The reviewer is becoming an editor of machine-generated feedback rather than the originator of it.

What It Means

For a platform like zipBoard, which already sits at the intersection of three industries that rarely share a tool, this is a rare structural advantage — you see review patterns across course builders, construction teams, and documentation leads that no single-vertical competitor can match. The opportunity is that the training data is sitting in your customers' annotation history right now.

The Smart Move

The sharpest operators are quietly shipping a "first-pass reviewer" — an AI layer that reads the SCORM module, the drawing set, or the help doc and pre-populates the comments a human reviewer would have written, flagged by confidence. They are not selling it as automation; they are selling it as giving the senior reviewer their Friday afternoon back. Pricing is shifting from seats to reviews-completed.
