What if the deliverable clients pay you for stops being the deliverable?
What's Shifting

Over the past few months, the strategy consulting world has quietly crossed a line. Mid-market clients are arriving at first meetings having already run their situation through Claude, Gemini or a custom GPT — and they're showing up with a synthesised SWOT, a competitor scan, and three strategic options before the engagement letter is signed. The work that used to anchor a strategy retainer is being done in the prep room, by the client, for free.

What It Means

For a firm like Thrive Strategy, this is both a threat and a quiet invitation. The threat: if your value sits in the slide deck, the framework, or the synthesis memo, that floor is dropping fast. The invitation: clients now arrive better-informed and more impatient, which means firms that can move straight to judgement, sequencing, and accountability — the parts AI still can't do credibly — command more pricing power, not less.

The Smart Move

The sharpest boutique strategy firms right now are repositioning their offer around what happens after the strategy exists — embedding alongside the leadership team for execution sprints, owning specific KPIs, and pricing on outcomes rather than artefacts. They're also publishing their proprietary points of view loudly, because in a world where anyone can generate a generic strategy in twenty minutes, having a distinctive, defensible philosophy is the new moat.

Three Signals to Watch
1
Clients arriving pre-analysed Procurement teams and founders are now routinely running RFP responses, market scans and competitor analyses through frontier models before briefing consultants. The "discovery phase" is collapsing into a single conversation.
2
Big four launching agentic strategy tools Deloitte, BCG and McKinsey have all rolled out internal AI agents this year that produce first-draft strategy work in hours rather than weeks. That pricing pressure is cascading down into the mid-market boutique tier within months, not years.
3
The rise of the fractional strategy operator A new class of independent advisors is winning work by bundling strategy with hands-on execution — sitting inside the business two days a week rather than delivering a report and leaving. Clients are increasingly choosing presence over polish.
